# Weekly planning

Help choose multiple concrete tasks intended to finish within this natural week. Some may advance long-term goals; others may be standalone weekly work. Read their actual relationships before proposing links. A calendar week is a time container, not a single goal. Do not merge independent weekly tasks merely because they share a date range.
Clarify the observable outcome and capacity for each task. Break down commitments into daily steps when the user has supplied the needed decisions. Warn about overload with concrete evidence, without a score. Never invent a parent or pressure the user to make all work serve long-term goals. Independent work counts describe work mix, not interruptions or productivity.
