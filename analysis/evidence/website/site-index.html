\n Task managers got it backward.\n

\n\n

\n They all promised you the same thing – that if you capture more, organize more, and keep up with more, you’ll finally get everything under control.\n

\n\n

\n I believe this is backward.\n

\n\n

\n When everything starts from an inbox, you’re reacting to things instead of spending time on goals that actually matter. You organize things. Process things. Jump between things. You feel busy, but not always productive.\n

\n\n

\n Yet there’s another way to work.\n

\n\n

With Hyperfocus, you start from “What do I want to achieve in the coming months?” It takes time to answer. But when you finally have it, the rest falls into place. You start feeling focused. Committed. Moving forward. Making progress toward goals that are actually yours.

\n\n

\n That's how I work. And if this resonates, I invite you to try Hyperfocus. Download the app, play around, and see if it fits.\n

\n \n \n Is Hyperfocus really free? What’s included?\n \n \n \n
\n

\n Yes. Hyperfocus is free forever. There is no Pro subscription, paid plan, or pack of AI credits to buy.\n

\n
\n
\n
\n \n \n How does AI work? Is it also free?\n \n \n \n
\n

\n All AI features use third-party AI providers. Hyperfocus includes AI for your first 14 days, so you can try every AI feature without setting anything up. After that, you can connect your own OpenRouter account, add a few dollars, and pay as you go. You pay the provider directly. I don’t take a cut or make money from your AI usage.\n

\n
\n
\n\n
\n \n \n What does Hyperfocus AI do, and can I use Hyperfocus without\n it?\n \n \n \n
\n

\n First, Hyperfocus AI reviews your goals\n and plans as you write, flagging vague and unrealistic plans. Like Grammarly, but for planning.\n

\n

\n Second, it includes purpose-built planning skills that help\n you clarify your priorities, choose what matters, and break\n goals down into workable steps.\n

\n

AI is optional. You can use the planner without it.

\n
\n
\n\n
\n \n \n Which operating systems does Hyperfocus support?\n \n \n \n
\n

Hyperfocus is currently available only for macOS.

\n
\n
\n\n
\n \n \n How is Hyperfocus different from a task manager or to-do app?\n \n \n \n
\n

\n Most task managers start with an inbox – they are great at helping you collect and organize tasks.\n

\n

\n Hyperfocus starts with your goals. It is great at identifying the outcomes that matter most and helping you consistently make progress toward them.\n

\n
\n
\n\n
\n \n Where is my data stored?\n \n \n
\n

\n Hyperfocus is local-first. Your goals, plans, and tasks are\n stored locally on your Mac, and the planner works offline.\n

\n

\n If you use AI, the parts of your plan relevant to your\n request may be sent to the third-party AI provider handling\n it, such as Google or OpenAI. For the included AI usage,\n requests are sent under a no-logging policy.\n

\n

\n If you connect your AI provider, that provider’s logging and\n data-handling policy applies.\n
